You can receive voicemail summaries directly in your inbox.  
This is especially convenient when working with a team:  
- If you're away from keyboard (AFK), your colleagues can read the summary and pick up the work.  
- Everyone gets a quick overview of the call without having to dial in.  
- Important information is stored in a shared place and easy to forward or archive.  

## Set up the Voice Mate app

> [!info] Getting Started  
> You'll need an email address where you want to receive the summaries.

1. **Add your email address**
   - Open the Voice Mate app.  
   - Go to _Integrations_ and select _Email_.  
   - Enter the email address where you want to receive the voicemail summaries.  
   - Save your settings.  

Once set up, you’ll start receiving voicemail summaries by email.
