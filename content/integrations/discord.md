Integrate Voice Mate with Discord to receive call summaries directly in your Discord channels.

## Set up the Voice Mate app

> [!info] Getting Started
> You'll need a Discord account.

1. **Create a Discord App**:
   Go to the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and click "New Application." Follow the steps to create your app.

2. **Obtain the Webhook URL**:
   In your Discord server, go to the channel where you want to receive call summaries. Click the gear icon to open channel settings, select "Integrations," then click "Webhooks" and "Create Webhook." Copy the generated Webhook URL.

3. **Add the Webhook to Voice Mate**:
   In your Voice Mate settings, paste the Webhook URL into the Discord integration section. This will enable Voice Mate to send call summaries to your chosen Discord channel.
