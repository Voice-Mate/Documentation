# Integration Bundle
Voice Mate offers seamless integrations with various tools and platforms to enhance your voicemail and callback automation. Below, you'll find details on how to connect Voice Mate with your favorite apps and services.

## Subscription supplement
The integration bundle will be charged on top of your normal subscription fee we a additional €4,99bundle. This amount is charged to your card when you add your first 3rd party integration and prorated for the period you have at least 1 integration enabled. This allows you to set up as many integrations as you desire for 1 fixed price

## Already Integrated

#### Calendar & Scheduling

- **Google Calendar** – Automatically schedule callbacks based on availability.
- **Microsoft Outlook Calendar** – Sync scheduled calls with your Outlook calendar.

#### Communication & Messaging

- **Slack** – Get notified in Slack channels when new voicemails arrive.
- **Telegram** – Get direct notifications of missed calls via Telegram bot.
- **Discord** – Get direct notifications of missed calls via Discord bot.


#### CRM & Sales Tools

- **HubSpot** – Log voicemail interactions and schedule follow-ups.

#### Productivity & Automation

- **Zapier** – Connect Voice Mate to thousands of apps for workflow automation.


#### Data & Reports
- **Webhooks** – Receive real-time updates about new voicemails, scheduled callbacks, and reports.

## Getting Started

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** section in your Voice Mate settings.
2. Choose the platform you want to connect.
3. Follow the on-screen setup instructions to authorize access.
4. Customize integration settings as needed.
